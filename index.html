Who is CDawg?

CDawgVA, whose real name is Connor, is a Twitch Streamer, YouTuber, voice actor, and voice actor. He was born on July 26, 1996 in Denbigh Wales. He was originally known by his anime voice impressions, prank calls, and voice actor reviews on his YouTube Channel. Over the years, however, he has done many different streams such as: travel vlogs, real-life videos across Japan, and high-profile charity events.
